## About

I'm someone who likes taking an idea from paper to something that actually runs and keeps running when things go wrong. That instinct has pulled me from traditional ML into agentic AI, RAG, and LLM systems, where the interesting problems aren't "can this model produce an answer" but "can I trust it, measure it, and let it act on its own"

What I want to help with is closing that gap making AI systems reliable enough that people can depend on them for real decisions, not just demos. I'm still early in that journey, learning mostly by building, breaking things, and fixing them properly instead of patching around them.

## What I'm Building Right Now

- **Agentic systems** that plan, call tools, and recover gracefully when a step fails not just single shot prompting
